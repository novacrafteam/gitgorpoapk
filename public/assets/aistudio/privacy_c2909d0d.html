Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

GorphinAI ("we", "our", "us"), developed by the Gorphin Team, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and store it, how we handle Google user data, and what rights you have when using https://gorphinai.pp.ua.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you register with email or sign in via Google OAuth or GitHub OAuth, we collect your email address, display name/username, and a hashed password (for email accounts) or OAuth verification status.
  • Google User Data Accessed: When you choose "Sign in with Google", GorphinAI requests only the minimal scopes: openid, email, and profile. We receive your Google email address and display name solely to create or authenticate your GorphinAI account.
  • Chat & Usage Data: Text prompts you submit, AI responses, chat titles, and daily quota counters (message count, reasoning tokens, and web/URL parse counts) necessary to provide the chat service and enforce plan limits.
  • Billing & Subscription Data: Selected subscription tier (Free, Basic, Plus, Pro), billing period, and payment order IDs processed through PayGate.to. We do not collect or store credit card numbers or banking credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity, maintain your active session, and secure your account.
  • To deliver AI text responses, save your chat history, and apply your plan's usage quotas.
  • To send account verification emails and respond to support requests.
  • To process subscription upgrades and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

GorphinAI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only use Google user data (email and basic profile name) to authenticate you and display your username inside GorphinAI.
  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data to train general-purpose AI/ML models.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with essential infrastructure providers strictly to operate the service:

  • Database Hosting (Turso / SQLite): Stores encrypted-in-transit account records, chat history, and quota logs.
  • Payment Processing (PayGate.to): Handles external checkout for paid subscriptions.
  • Email Delivery: Used solely to deliver account verification emails when enabled.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and chat data for as long as your account remains active. Session tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage and expire automatically or when you sign out. You may delete individual chats at any time inside the app, or request full account and data deletion by emailing support@gorphinai.pp.ua.

6. Security

All traffic between your browser and GorphinAI is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashes, and OAuth tokens are never exposed to third-party scripts.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact the Gorphin Team at support@gorphinai.pp.ua.
